Language items are verbal and single-screen. Use only common nouns from the stated referent
classes. Every word you use is looked up in a per-locale lexical database and checked against
the levers you were given (frequency band, age of acquisition, imageability), and the semantic
relation between options is checked against a category tree. Keep all options the same part of
speech, similar in length and similar in frequency, so that the options alone do not reveal the key.
In semantic_odd_one_out the other words must form one clean group: either all from the same
subcategory, or each from a different subcategory of one category. Never let two of them share a
subcategory that the third does not (for example carrot and turnip with cabbage), because then a
second option is also odd. Fill lever_realisation with each lever name and the value you realised.
